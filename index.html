/01 — FIRST MESSAGE TO SHOWCASE

FROM DM
TO SHOWCASE

This network doesn't take scripts from strangers. It takes scripts that walked the full road — a direct message, three independent votes, a showcase recording, and a Discord announcement. If any step fails, the case is closed with a written reason. There is no form to fill — the first message is the whole beginning.

  1. 01
    MESSAGE ONE OF THE THREEContact any one approver through their channel's About page or business email. Tell them what you built. That message opens the case — the first yes opens the door to the rest.
  2. 02
    FIRST REVIEWThe approver you messaged checks the script: does it work, who made it, do you have permission to share it, is the source clean.
  3. 03
    THE OTHER TWO VOTESIf vote one passes, the case goes to the remaining two channels. Each votes independently. No group pressure, no shortcut.
  4. 04
    3/3 OR CLOSEDUnanimous yes → the script moves to showcase. Any "no" → the case is closed and the reason is written down. No drama, no re-litigating.
  5. 05
    SHOWCASE + ANNOUNCEA partner channel records the showcase with full credit, and the result is announced across the two Discord servers.
